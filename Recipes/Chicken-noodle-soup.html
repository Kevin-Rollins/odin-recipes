Chicken Noodle Soup

This might rival your mamas chicken noodle soup and it will definilty warm up your body and tastebuds at the same time.

Ingredients

  • Rotisserie chicken
  • Seasonings: Salt,pepper, bullion cubs
  • Water
  • Carrots
  • Cellery
  • Onions
  • egg noodles

Directions

  1. Boil water. This will be your base so dont skimp
  2. Chop veggies and add in once water is boiling
  3. Add in bullion cubes
  4. Add in noodles
  5. Add in chicken
  6. Season to taste
