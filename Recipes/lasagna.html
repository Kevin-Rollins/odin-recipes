Lasagna Recipe

Description

This may not be your grandmothers lasagna but its darn close. If you've ever wondered how to make a lasagna that will have people making togo plates then look no further.

Ingredients

  • noodles
  • ricotta cheese
  • spices:salt, pepper,parsely
  • tomatoe paste
  • tomatoe sauce
  • your choice of meat i.e. ground beef,turkey etc..

Steps

  1. lay noodles in oven save pan and odere them so theey cover the bottom uniformley
  2. Spread a layer of riccotta on
  3. cook the meat
  4. Drain meat and mix in the tomatoe paste and tomato sauce
  5. Add layer meat sauce to pan.
  6. Then repeat that pattern till the pan is full
